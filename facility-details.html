Overview

Dreamscape Residences by Urban Habitat is located in Sector 25A, Noida West, sprawling across an expansive 12-acre campus. This premium project offers thoughtfully designed 2 BHK, 2 BHK + study, 3 BHK, and 3 BHK + study apartments, all ready for immediate possession. Situated at a prime junction connecting Greater Noida and Ghaziabad, Dreamscape Residences ensures seamless connectivity to key areas and highways, making it a top choice for stress-free commuting. Its modern architecture and exceptional amenities provide the elevated lifestyle you’ve always envisioned. With 3-side open apartments, residents can bask in abundant sunlight and enjoy a fresh breeze every day. The project includes a wide range of facilities such as a grand amphitheater, an infinity swimming pool, a cycling and jogging track, and a boutique shopping plaza. The family clubhouse is equipped with state-of-the-art recreational and sports amenities, all set amidst a beautifully landscaped, eco-friendly environment, designed to make everyday living a delight. Moreover, the area is well-connected to reputed hospitals and renowned educational institutions, ensuring the ultimate convenience and comfort for residents.
